# Settra: founder-led LinkedIn outreach playbook

## The first ICP to learn from

Start with **operations-led companies of roughly 20–250 people whose essential
workflows still live in Google Sheets**. Their teams want to use ChatGPT,
Claude, or automation for recurring reporting, but they cannot trust an AI
assistant to infer business definitions or cope with a changing spreadsheet.

Prioritize the person closest to the pain rather than the most senior person:

- Operations Manager / Head of Operations / COO
- Chief of Staff
- RevOps / Sales Operations leader
- FP&A / Finance Operations lead
- Agency owner or Director of Delivery

This is deliberately a learning ICP, not a permanent market definition. Give
yourself the first 30–50 relevant conversations to prove which workflow, buyer,
and company profile reacts most strongly.

## What Settra is selling in one sentence

Settra turns a team’s spreadsheet data into a reliable, reusable data App so AI
assistants can answer recurring questions with the right definitions and rules
instead of guessing.

Do not lead with “MCP,” “semantic layer,” or “agent-native” in a cold message.
Those explain how it works after the prospect recognizes their own reporting
problem. Lead with the repeated question they keep rebuilding from a Sheet.

## Sales Navigator starting filters

- Geography: where you can comfortably sell and support.
- Company headcount: 11–50, 51–200, then test 201–500 separately.
- Seniority: Manager, Director, VP, CXO, Owner.
- Titles: `operations`, `chief of staff`, `revenue operations`, `sales operations`, `FP&A`, `finance operations`, `delivery`.
- Keywords: `Google Sheets`, `reporting`, `forecasting`, `pipeline`, `operations`, `automation`, `AI`.
- Spot-check every profile. A search match is not a reason to message somebody.

Keep a small batch: research 10–15 people, send 5–10 thoughtful connection
requests, wait, then refine. A meaningful response is more valuable than
volume while the message is still unproven.

## Signals worth recording

1. A concrete spreadsheet workflow: pipeline review, target-vs-actual,
   operations tracker, customer list, forecast, client status report.
2. A current AI/automation initiative, or an announced hiring need in ops,
   RevOps, finance ops, or analytics.
3. A recent growth, new service, migration, or process change that makes
   reporting definitions harder to maintain.
4. A clear “reporting owner” title and enough company scale for repetitive
   questions to interrupt real work.

The useful note is specific: “Hiring RevOps analyst; CEO posts a weekly Sheets
pipeline review.” “Uses ChatGPT” is not enough by itself.

## Message sequence

### 1. Connection request (under 300 characters)

> Hi {first_name} — saw {specific signal}. I’m speaking with ops teams that
> still run key workflows in Sheets but want to use AI without letting it guess
> at the data. Would be glad to connect.

### 2. After they accept

> Thanks for connecting, {first_name}. Quick question: when someone asks a
> recurring question about {workflow}, does the team still rebuild the answer
> from the spreadsheet each time, or do you have a dependable workflow for it
> already?

### 3. If the problem resonates

> That’s the gap we’re building Settra for: AI assistants can answer from the
> team’s Sheets using the same definitions and rules the team trusts—without raw
> access or improvisation. Would a 15-minute look at your {workflow} be useful?

### 4. One gentle follow-up (about 5–7 business days later)

> Just resurfacing this in case {workflow} is on your list. If not, no worries
> at all—I’m mainly trying to understand which spreadsheet-based questions teams
> most want AI to answer reliably.

Do not automate LinkedIn messages or scrape profiles. Apart from platform risk,
the advantage in this early phase is making a real, relevant observation.

## What to measure each Friday

| Metric                                    | Why it matters                               |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Profiles researched → connection requests | Is the list viable?                          |
| Connection acceptance rate                | Is the personalization and profile credible? |
| Accepted → meaningful replies             | Does the problem framing land?               |
| Meaningful replies → calls                | Is the offer concrete enough?                |
| Exact workflow and wording used           | What should the next batch focus on?         |

A "no" that tells you they use a warehouse, have no recurring question, or
already solved the workflow is evidence—record it and refine the ICP.
